CityWalker privacy policy

What the app does

CityWalker records where you walk, cycle or drive, shows the streets you have covered on a map, and keeps your history and coins on your phone.

What it collects and why

  • Precise location. Used while you record, and while the map is open to show your position, to match your position to streets. Recording continues with the screen off through a notification until you stop it.
  • Physical activity type (walking, cycling, in a vehicle). Provided by Google Play services on your phone and used to tell the travel modes apart. Without this permission the app uses your speed instead.
  • A name you choose and the avatar you select. Used only inside the app.

Where it is stored

Your covered streets, history, routes, coins, skins, settings and profile are stored only on your phone. The developer does not receive them. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. You can delete your history and progress in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes everything. If device backup is turned on in Android, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup.

What goes over the internet

  • Map pictures (tiles) are requested from the tile server in Settings > Map servers (OpenStreetMap's by default). That server can see your IP address and which part of the map you view.
  • Street data is downloaded from an Overpass server (public by default, or the one you set) for the area you choose, and, while you record outside your downloaded map, for the area around you. That server can see your IP address and the area requested.
  • Location and activity recognition are handled by Google Play services under Google's own privacy policy.

Map data is from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Health Connect (optional)

If you choose "Send recordings to Health Connect" in Settings, the app writes the start time, end time and distance of your walks and rides to Health Connect on your phone, where other apps you allow can read them. The app never reads anything from Health Connect.

Purchases

The optional Supporter pack is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app only learns whether it has been bought. It unlocks extra icons and nothing else.

Pictures you share

The "Share a picture of my progress" button makes a picture on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. Nothing is uploaded by CityWalker.

Backups and reports

Backup files and map files you export are created only when you choose to, and saved where you choose. If the app closes unexpectedly it saves a short technical report on the phone. It is sent only if you choose to send it, and it contains no location data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app.
